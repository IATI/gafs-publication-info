************
Support
************

If your organization requires assistance implementing these technical requirements, the following support channels are available:

* **IATI Support Team**: For general technical guidance on the IATI Standard or the tagging process, visit the `IATI Contact page <https://iatistandard.org/en/guidance/get-support/>`_.
* **GAFS-Specific Support**: For questions specifically regarding GAFS Preparedness Plans and data integration, you can reach out to the dedicated support email at gafs@iatistandard.org.
* **Alternative Arrangements**: If it is not technically feasible for your organization to tag or annotate IATI activities through the standard fields, please notify `IATI Support directly <https://iatistandard.org/en/guidance/get-support/>`_. They can help identify which of your activities are relevant to a Preparedness Plan and discuss alternative arrangements to ensure your data is captured.
